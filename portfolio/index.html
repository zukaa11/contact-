WHAT I CAN BUILD FOR YOU

Robotics Projects I Build with C++ & Microcontrollers

Here is a simple look at the real-world machines, robots, and smart automated devices I can program and assemble.

1. Autonomous Mobile Robots

Self-driving wheeled robots that navigate autonomously. Using optical infrared sensors, ultrasonic distance sensors, and C++ mathematical control formulas (called PID loops), the robot stays centered on tracks, follows lines, and steers around obstacles without human intervention.

Line Followers Obstacle Avoidance Motor Speed Tuning

2. Smart Home & Safety Systems

Automated home controllers powered by small computer chips (like ESP32). They monitor room temperature, detect smoke or gas leaks, sound alarms, turn on ventilation fans, and switch electrical lights or appliances on and off safely.

Gas & Smoke Detection Relay Switches OLED Displays

3. RFID Gates & Parking Barriers

Electronic security gates and automated parking barriers. A person can tap an authorized RFID card, or a car can pull up to an ultrasonic sensor. The system verifies the identity, unlocks the electric lock, lifts the barrier arm, and counts open parking spots on a screen.

RFID Keycard Scanner Motorized Gate Arm Parking Spot Counter

4. Smart Traffic Light Controllers

Intelligent four-way traffic lights programmed with strict safety rules. They sequence green, yellow, and red lights in an exact order. When a pedestrian presses a crosswalk button, the code safely changes the traffic lights so people can cross without danger.

Intersection Timing Pedestrian Crosswalk Fail-Safe Logic
